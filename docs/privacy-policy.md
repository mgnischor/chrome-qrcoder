# QRCoder Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 24, 2026

QRCoder is a Chrome extension that creates QR codes for web addresses, text and Wi-Fi networks on your
device. This policy explains what the extension does with your information.

## Summary

- QRCoder **does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data**.
- Everything happens locally in your browser. The extension has no server, no account system, no
  analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.
- What you type and the QR code made from it are shown to you and discarded when you close the popup,
  unless you leave the optional "remember what I typed" setting on, which keeps it in browser memory only.

## What QRCoder does

QRCoder turns the link, text or Wi-Fi details you type into a QR code, draws it in the popup, and lets you
copy it to the clipboard or save it as a PNG or SVG image. The "Use current page" button fills the link
field with the address of the tab you are on.

## Information we collect

None. The developer never receives any information from the extension: no links, no text, no Wi-Fi names
or passwords, no browsing activity, no page content, no clipboard content, no device or usage data.

## Information stored on your device

| Information                                                                                              | Where it is stored                                                   | Purpose                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Your preferences (tab last used, error correction level, image size, whether to remember what you typed) | `chrome.storage.sync`                                                | Remember your settings between sessions. Contains nothing you typed into the fields.           |
| What you typed (link, text, Wi-Fi name, password, security type), **only if "remember" is on**            | `chrome.storage.session`: browser memory only, never written to disk | Still be there when you reopen the popup. Erased when Chrome closes.                            |

Details:

- **Preferences and Chrome Sync.** `chrome.storage.sync` is a feature of Chrome. If you are signed in to
  Chrome with sync enabled, Chrome may sync these preferences across your devices through your Google
  account. That is handled by Google under its own privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.
- **What you typed.** The setting is on by default. The text is kept unencrypted in browser memory,
  **including a Wi-Fi password if you entered one**, and is erased when Chrome closes. Turning the
  setting off, or choosing **Clear all fields**, deletes it immediately. With the setting off, nothing you
  type is kept after the popup closes.
- **Generated images.** They are never stored by the extension. They exist while the popup is open, and
  in the files and clipboard content you create yourself with **Save** and **Copy**.

## Clipboard and downloads

When you press **Copy image**, QRCoder writes the image to your clipboard. When you press **Save PNG** or
**Save SVG**, it starts a normal browser download. Both happen only in response to your click and need no
special permission. QRCoder never reads your clipboard.

## Web page content

QRCoder does not read, collect or transmit the content of the pages you visit. When you open the popup
from the toolbar icon or the keyboard shortcut, Chrome's `activeTab` permission gives the extension
temporary access to that tab, and the extension reads **only its address**, and only when you press
**Use current page**. It requests no permission to access websites in the background and has no content
scripts.

## Permissions

| Permission  | Why QRCoder needs it                                                                                         |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `storage`   | Save your preferences, and hold what you typed in memory while the browser is open.                          |
| `activeTab` | Read the address of the current tab for the **Use current page** button, when you open the popup on that tab. |

## Third parties

QRCoder does not use third-party services, SDKs, advertising or analytics, and it does not sell or share
data with anyone. Its use of information complies with the
[Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Security

QR codes are generated by code packaged in the extension. Because the extension never sends data off your
device, there is nothing for the developer to store or leak. Anyone who can see a QR code can read what it
contains: a Wi-Fi QR code includes the network password in a form any scanner can read, so share it only
with people you trust. You remain responsible for keeping your device and browser profile secure.

## Children's privacy

QRCoder is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect
information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change (for example, if a future version stores what you typed on disk or
connects to a service), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective
date above will change.
